IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- The gameplay table is baked into the gameplay environment backgrounds; do not layer a legacy separate table asset over them.
- Use the mobile gameplay environment for portrait/mobile and the desktop gameplay environment for wide screens.
- Use the neutral action-button shell for Fold/Check/Call/Bet/Raise/All-In; color/state/text should be live CSS/DOM.
- Use the standard seat ticket for normal opponents and the hero/active ticket for the local/active player treatment.
- Do not generate another asset family unless a real implementation gap is proven.
